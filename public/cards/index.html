Business cards

Four seasonal fronts, each showing Levi on that season's headline job, plus a year-round Classic. Every design shares one back: contact details, every service with the season's pick on top, and a QR code to the website.

Sending them to the printer

Card
3.5 × 2 in: the US standard size. Double-sided. The front sells the season; the back is the reference card people keep.
Files
One PDF per design with two pages, front then back, each 3.75 × 2.25 in. That includes a 0.125 in bleed on every side, which the printer trims off. Fonts are embedded and the artwork is 300+ ppi.
Color
Upload the RGB PDF unless the printer asks for CMYK. Dark purples and blues print a little darker than they look on screen, so order a printed proof first.
Paper
16 pt matte or soft-touch feels premium and takes a pen. Spot UV on the logo is a nice upgrade if it's offered. Standard (square) corners.
Quantity
250–500 of each seasonal card, ordered a few weeks before its season. The Classic works year-round for everything else.
